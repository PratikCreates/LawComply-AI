# ACME Finance Operations Policy

The finance team prepares indirect tax filings and stores support in shared folders. Reconciliation is performed monthly, but approval evidence is not consistently captured in the same system used for filing.

Critical journal entries are reviewed verbally by the controller before posting. The ERP allows manual overrides for certain tax mappings, and those changes are tracked in email.

ACME relies on an external payroll processor and a local filing agent in two jurisdictions. Vendor due diligence is completed at onboarding, but there is no annual control review or documented fallback plan if either provider misses a statutory deadline.

Regulatory updates are monitored informally through newsletters and partner emails. No single role is accountable for monitoring tax rule changes.
