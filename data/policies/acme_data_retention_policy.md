# ACME Data Retention and Privacy Policy

ACME collects customer profile information, support correspondence, and billing identifiers. The company states that personal data should be kept only as long as needed for operational use, but specific retention periods are not centrally published. Customer deletion requests are handled by the support team through a shared mailbox.

Vendor onboarding requires procurement review, but privacy addenda are requested only for high-risk vendors. There is no standard checklist for processor clauses or deletion terms at offboarding.

The security team maintains access controls for production systems and runs annual disaster recovery testing. Breach escalation guidance exists in the incident response runbook, though the policy does not define regulatory notification timelines.

Business units maintain their own records of processing activities in spreadsheets. There is no unified inventory and no quarterly control review.
