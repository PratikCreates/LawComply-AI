# Financial Controls Guidance

## TAX-CTRL-01
Organizations shall document ownership for tax filings, reconciliation workflows, and review sign-offs for all statutory submissions.

## TAX-CTRL-02
Source records supporting tax positions shall be retained according to jurisdictional retention schedules and be retrievable for audit.

## TAX-CTRL-03
Material manual journal entries affecting tax, revenue recognition, or statutory reporting shall require secondary approval and evidence of review.

## TAX-CTRL-04
Third-party payroll, accounting, or filing agents shall be subject to due diligence, defined service responsibilities, and periodic control reviews.

## TAX-CTRL-05
Changes to tax rates, reporting rules, or jurisdictional obligations shall be tracked through a formal regulatory monitoring process with assigned accountability.

## TAX-CTRL-06
Organizations shall test business continuity procedures for statutory reporting systems and preserve a fallback process for filing deadlines.
