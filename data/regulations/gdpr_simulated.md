# GDPR Simulated Guidance

## GDPR-ART-05
Personal data shall be processed lawfully, fairly, and transparently. Organizations must maintain clear purposes, apply data minimization, and ensure storage limitation controls.

## GDPR-ART-13
When personal data is collected from a data subject, the controller shall provide notice describing processing purposes, retention periods, recipient categories, and complaint channels.

## GDPR-ART-17
Organizations shall support deletion and erasure requests without undue delay unless a lawful exemption applies. Retention exceptions must be documented.

## GDPR-ART-24
Controllers shall implement appropriate technical and organizational measures and review those measures periodically to demonstrate compliance.

## GDPR-ART-28
Processors shall be governed by binding contracts that define confidentiality, sub-processing restrictions, breach support, and deletion or return of personal data at termination.

## GDPR-ART-30
Controllers and processors shall maintain records of processing activities, including purpose, categories of data, recipients, transfers, and retention schedules.

## GDPR-ART-32
Organizations shall implement risk-based security controls including confidentiality, integrity, resilience, access control, and tested restoration procedures.

## GDPR-ART-33
Personal data breaches shall be assessed promptly and reported to the supervisory authority without undue delay where notification obligations apply.
